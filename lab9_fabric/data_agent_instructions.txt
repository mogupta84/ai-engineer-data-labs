Tickets that failed a quality rule are in tickets_quarantine, not in tickets_clean. tickets_quarantine has one row per parked ticket, so count its rows for the total; failed_rules names each problem the ticket has, separated by '; '. To count one reason, count the rows whose failed_rules contains its name; a ticket with two problems counts once under each, so those counts can add up to more than the total. 'category' is what support recorded; 'ai_category' is the AI label.
