FreshCart Customer Refund Policy
Version 3.0 · Effective 01 April 2024 · Supersedes v2.0 (2022)

1. Scope

This policy governs refunds for food delivery orders placed through the FreshCart consumer application. It applies
to all orders in India from the effective date above. Restaurant partner liability is governed separately by the Partner
Agreement.
2. Eligible reasons for refund

A customer is eligible to request a refund where the order was not delivered, was delivered materially incomplete,
arrived in a spilled or damaged condition, or where the items delivered do not match the items ordered.
2.1 Spillage and damage

Spillage means leakage of liquid or semi-solid contents from the packaging such that the item is unfit for
consumption. Partial leakage that does not render the item unfit is assessed as damage and attracts a partial
refund of up to fifty percent of item value. Photographic evidence must be submitted within 24 hours of delivery.
3. Claim frequency limits

A customer may make up to three refund claims in any rolling thirty day period. A fourth and any subsequent claim
within that period is automatically declined by the system and must be escalated to a support supervisor for manual
review. This limit was raised from two claims under v2.0 of this policy.
4. Refund timelines

Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within five to seven working days. Wallet credit,
where the customer elects it, is applied immediately.
5. Exclusions

Refunds are not available where the customer provided an incorrect delivery address, where delivery was refused
without cause, or where the claim is made more than 48 hours after the delivery timestamp.
